Builds a 1MB Kickstart: a second 512 KB bank at $E00000 is added and the ROM is patched to scan it. Modules you pick from a donor ROM are moved into that bank, the original is switched off and its priority is kept. For Hyperion Kickstarts (3.1.4, 3.2) on A500+/A600 (one 27C800) and A1200 (two 27C400). Not tested on real hardware — try the result in an emulator first.
