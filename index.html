Tick the changes you want. Each one asks for the files it needs, taken from your own copies of the software, and anything beyond 512 KB goes in a second 512 KB area at $E00000. That makes a 1 MB image: one 27C800 for A500+/A600, or two 27C400 (HI/LO) for the A1200. The second area works on Hyperion Kickstarts (3.1.4, 3.2), 3.1 (40.68) and 2.05 (37.350); on the older ones the scan table has no room to grow, so the scan of $F00000–$F80000 (CDTV-style expansion ROMs) is dropped to make room. Not tested on real hardware — try the result in an emulator first.

Advanced: replace or add any module

A replacement with the same name as an existing module overwrites it in place when it fits in the old space, and the ROM keeps its size. Anything that doesn’t fit, and any new module, goes in the extended area. Use module files saved from the Modules tab, or Amiga executables that contain a resident tag.
